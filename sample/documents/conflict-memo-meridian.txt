THORNE VAUX LLP
RISK AND COMPLIANCE

CONFLICT CHECK MEMORANDUM

Date:        6 March 2026
Prepared by: Rita Okonjo, Head of Risk
Matter:      NTL-2026-0114 — proposed engagement for Northwind Trading Limited
Circulated:  Perrine Duval (partner), Sian Aldridge (compliance)

1. PROPOSED ENGAGEMENT

Northwind Trading Limited has asked the firm to act in a charterparty dispute against
Calder Shipping AG. Perrine Duval would supervise.

2. SEARCH CARRIED OUT

The conflicts database was searched for Northwind Trading Limited, Calder Shipping AG, and
the vessel MV Aurelia, together with the corporate groups of each counterparty.

3. FINDINGS

3.1 The firm acts for Meridian Bulk Carriers SA on a secured lending facility. That
engagement is led by James Trelawney and has been open since 11 September 2024.

3.2 Meridian Bulk Carriers SA holds 18 per cent of the issued share capital of Calder
Shipping AG. Meridian is not a party to the charterparty and has no role in the dispute.

3.3 The firm has never acted for Calder Shipping AG.

4. ANALYSIS

Acting for Northwind against Calder does not place the firm in opposition to a current
client. Meridian is a shareholder in the opposing party, not the opposing party itself.
There is no retainer with Calder and therefore no duty of loyalty owed to it.

The residual risk is confidential information. Mr Trelawney's team holds financial
information about Meridian which could, in principle, be relevant to Calder's ability to
satisfy an award. That information must not reach the disputes team.

5. DECISION

The engagement may be accepted subject to an information barrier between the Meridian
lending team and the Northwind disputes team. Specifically:

(a) Kavi Iyer, Adaeze Mensah and Perrine Duval are screened from the Meridian matter.
(b) James Trelawney is screened from matter NTL-2026-0114.
(c) Neither team may discuss the other's matter, including informally.

This decision is to be reviewed if Meridian's shareholding in Calder changes materially, or
if Meridian is joined to the proceedings.

Rita Okonjo
